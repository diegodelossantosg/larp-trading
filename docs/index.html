How it works

  1. Once a day (weekdays, about 17:15 Spain time, while the US market is open), the AI wakes up.
  2. It reads the news, searches the web for what is moving markets (central banks, politics, wars, big company news) and studies recent price charts of about 75 ETFs.
  3. It decides how to split the money: for example "70% S&P 500, 20% energy, 10% gold". Money it has no strong idea for stays in the S&P 500.
  4. Safety rules in the code then check the plan (max 30% in one ETF, at most 8 ETFs, small limits on risky ETFs) and place the trades.
  5. It does not trade all day long. Most days it changes little or nothing. Doing nothing is often the smart move.
  6. After the market closes, it writes down how much the money is worth, so this page can compare it with the S&P 500.

Words you'll see

S&P 500 / SPY
The 500 biggest US companies. SPY is an ETF that buys all of them. It's the benchmark: if the AI can't beat it, you'd be better off just buying SPY.
ETF
"Exchange-traded fund". One thing you can buy that holds many stocks, bonds or commodities at once, like a ready-made basket.
Paper trading
Practice trading with fake money at real prices.
Return
How much the money grew or shrank, as a percentage. +5% on $1,000 means $50 profit.
Biggest dip
The largest drop from a high point to a low point along the way. It shows how bumpy the ride was.
Confidence
How sure the AI said it was about that day's decision (low / medium / high).
